description=[h1]🛢️ Barrels are no longer just decoration[/h1]
description=
description=Knox Country is full of industrial drums, military barrels, and forgotten containers. In vanilla, most of them are just scenery. With [b]Barrels Expanded[/b], they become part of your survival story.
description=
description=Crack one open and you might find clean water, tainted water, gasoline, bleach, or nothing at all. Maybe it saves your group. Maybe it gives your base a new fuel reserve. Maybe it is just another gamble in a world that already hates you.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h1]👁️ What does it add to your world?[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Useful Exploration:[/b] Search industrial, military, and crafted barrels for resources instead of walking past decoration.
description=[*] [b]Emergency Water:[/b] Drink from water barrels when thirst becomes a problem.
description=[*] [b]Hygiene Anywhere:[/b] Wash yourself, clothes, and washable items using barrel water.
description=[*] [b]Liquid Storage:[/b] Pour compatible liquids into barrels or fill containers from them.
description=[*] [b]Progressive Emptying:[/b] Drain unwanted contents over time; if you stop halfway, the drained amount stays drained.
description=[*] [b]Base Logistics:[/b] Pick up, move, and place opened barrels while preserving their contents and dynamic weight.
description=[*] [b]Scavenger Tools:[/b] Crowbars, screwdrivers, funnels, hoses, and similar tools give barrel use a grounded survival feel.
description=[*] [b]Solo and Multiplayer:[/b] Works for singleplayer runs and multiplayer bases.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h1]🧰 Current liquids and tools[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Liquids:[/b] Water, Tainted Water, Gasoline, Bleach, and Empty barrels.
description=[*] [b]Open tools:[/b] Crowbar, Forged Crowbar, Screwdriver, Pipe Wrench, or Sheet Metal Snips.
description=[*] [b]Transfer tools:[/b] Funnel to pour into barrels, Rubber Hose to fill containers from barrels.
description=[*] [b]Optional Mod Support:[/b] DamnLib/USMIL military gas and water cans work with barrel liquid transfers.
description=[/list]

description=[h1]❓ Save FAQ[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b]
description=Yes. World barrels become interactive as you explore and load the map, no new world required.
description=
description=[b]What happens if I remove it later?[/b]
description=The mod adds no custom maps or mandatory new items. If disabled, barrels go back to being decoration. To avoid inventory oddities, place down any barrels you are carrying before disabling it.

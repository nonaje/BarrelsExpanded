description=[h1]English[/h1]
description=
description=[h1]Barrels are no longer just decoration.[/h1]
description=
description=Knox Country is full of industrial drums, military stockpiles, forgotten garages and suspicious backroom storage. In vanilla, most of those barrels are just scenery.
description=
description=[b]Barrels Expanded[/b] turns them into persistent, multiplayer-safe liquid containers with real gameplay value. Crack one open and you might find clean water, tainted water, gasoline, bleach... or nothing but disappointment and rust.
description=
description=[h2]What it adds[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Open and inspect supported world barrels.
description=[*]Drink from water barrels when desperate enough.
description=[*]Wash yourself, clothing and washable items using barrel water.
description=[*]Pour compatible liquids into barrels.
description=[*]Extract liquids into compatible containers.
description=[*]Empty barrels completely.
description=[*]Pick up and place opened barrels while preserving contents, ID and weight.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Built for multiplayer[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Server-authoritative validation for real state changes.
description=[*]Persistent modData that survives saves and restarts.
description=[*]Transfer locks to prevent players fighting over the same barrel.
description=[*]Sync fixes for washing visuals, item replacement, moveables and stalled transfers.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Gameplay details[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Supported liquids: Water, Tainted Water, Gasoline and Bleach.
description=[*]Barrel capacity: 160 units.
description=[*]Dynamic weight based on liquid type and amount.
description=[*]Requires tools for some interactions: crowbar/screwdriver/etc. to open, funnel to pour, hose to extract.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=[*]Singleplayer supported.
description=[*]Multiplayer supported.
description=[*]Client and server must both load the mod.
description=[/list]

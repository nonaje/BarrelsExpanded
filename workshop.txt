description=[b]Que pasa si lo saco despues?[/b]
description=El mod no agrega mapas, tiles propios ni items nuevos obligatorios. Si lo desactivas, los barriles dejan de tener las interacciones de Barrels Expanded y vuelven a comportarse como objetos vanilla/decoracion.
description=
description=Los datos guardados por el mod pueden quedar en el save como modData inactivo. No deberian romper la partida, pero el contenido de los barriles no va a estar disponible mientras el mod este desactivado. Si pensas removerlo, lo mas prolijo es colocar en el mundo cualquier barril que tengas encima, guardar, cerrar el juego/servidor y recien ahi desactivar el mod.

description=[h1]English[/h1]
description=
description=[h1]Barrels are no longer just decoration.[/h1]
description=
description=Knox Country is full of industrial drums, military stockpiles, forgotten garages and suspicious backroom storage. In vanilla, most of those barrels are just scenery.
description=
description=[b]Barrels Expanded[/b] turns them into persistent, multiplayer-safe liquid containers with real gameplay value. Crack one open and you might find clean water, tainted water, gasoline, bleach... or nothing but disappointment and rust.
description=
description=[h2]What it adds[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Open and inspect supported world barrels.
description=[*]Drink from water barrels when desperate enough.
description=[*]Wash yourself, clothing and washable items using barrel water.
description=[*]Pour compatible liquids into barrels.
description=[*]Extract liquids into compatible containers.
description=[*]Empty barrels completely.
description=[*]Pick up and place opened barrels while preserving contents, ID and weight.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Built for multiplayer[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Server-authoritative validation for real state changes.
description=[*]Persistent modData that survives saves and restarts.
description=[*]Transfer locks to prevent players fighting over the same barrel.
description=[*]Sync fixes for washing visuals, item replacement, moveables and stalled transfers.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Gameplay details[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Supported liquids: Water, Tainted Water, Gasoline and Bleach.
description=[*]Barrel capacity: 160 units.
description=[*]Dynamic weight based on liquid type and amount.
description=[*]Requires tools for some interactions: crowbar/screwdriver/etc. to open, funnel to pour, hose to extract.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=[*]Singleplayer supported.
description=[*]Multiplayer supported.
description=[*]Client and server must both load the mod.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Save FAQ[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b]
description=Yes. Barrels Expanded is designed to be added to existing saves. Compatible vanilla barrels become interactive when their area is loaded; no new world is required.
description=
description=As always with mods, especially in multiplayer, make a save backup before enabling it.
description=
description=[b]What happens if I remove it later?[/b]
description=The mod does not add custom maps, custom tiles or required custom item types. If you disable it, barrels stop using Barrels Expanded interactions and go back to behaving like vanilla world objects.
description=
description=The mod's saved modData may remain in the save but becomes inactive. It should not break the save, but barrel contents managed by the mod will not be usable while the mod is disabled. Before removing it, place any carried barrels back into the world, save, shut down the game/server, and then disable the mod.
description=
description=Version 1.0.0 is the first public release. If you find a sync issue, a broken barrel tile, or a container that should be supported, drop a report with the item/barrel name and what happened.

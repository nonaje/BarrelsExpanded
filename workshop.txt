description=[h1]🛢️ Barrels are no longer just decoration[/h1]
description=
description=Tired of seeing hundreds of industrial barrels scattered across the map that only serve to get in your way? In the vanilla game, they are just static scenery. With [b]Barrels Expanded[/b], every single one becomes a real, functional, and strategic resource vital for your survival—whether you are playing solo or on multiplayer servers.
description=
description=When you crack one open, you take a gamble: will it be clean water to live another day, fuel for your escape vehicle, bleach... or just rust and disappointment? The old civilization collapsed and left its reserves behind. In Knox Country, every drop counts. Will you ignore them or will you loot them?

description=[h1]👁️ What can you do?[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Active Exploration:[/b] Search and interact with industrial drums and barrels all over the world.
description=[*] [b]Emergency Resources:[/b] Drink directly from water barrels when dehydration hits hard.
description=[*] [b]Total Hygiene:[/b] Wash your body or clean your bloody clothes using the collected water.
description=[*] [b]Fluid Management:[/b] Pour or extract compatible liquids (Water, Tainted Water, Fuel, and Bleach) using your own containers.
description=[*] [b]Maintenance:[/b] Empty barrels completely if you need to clean them out or change their contents.
description=[*] [b]Survival Logistics:[/b] Pick up, transport, and place barrels in your base while preserving their exact contents and dynamic weight.
description=[*] [b]Scavenger Tools:[/b] Some actions will require tools like crowbars, screwdrivers, funnels, or hoses to get the job done.
description=[/list]

description=[h1]❓ Save FAQ[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b]
description=Yes. World barrels will automatically become interactive as you explore and load the map, no new world required. As the unwritten law of the apocalypse goes: [i]always backup your save before enabling any mod.[/i]
description=
description=[b]What happens if I remove it later?[/b]
description=This mod is clean; it adds no custom maps or mandatory new items. If disabled, barrels simply go back to being harmless decoration without breaking your save. To avoid any issues, we recommend placing down any barrels you are carrying in your inventory before disabling it.
